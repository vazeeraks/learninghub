Our Story

EduTutor wasn't born in a boardroom. It started with a shared frustration.

We were students in Kochi, watching friends drop out of subjects they were smart enough to pass. Not from lack of effort — but because the way it was taught just didn't click. 10-hour YouTube playlists. Textbooks that read like encyclopedias. Coaching classes that moved too fast.

So in 2024, we asked a simple question: What if learning actually made sense the first time?

We started small. One whiteboard, one phone camera, and one rule: explain it like you would to a friend at 11 PM before an exam. No jargon. No ego. Just clear concepts, step-by-step.

The first 50 students we helped told 5 more each. Soon we weren't just teaching — we were building trust.

Today, EduTutor is that same idea, scaled up. We're still students at heart. We still test every lesson on real learners before we publish it. And we still believe the best teacher is the one who remembers what it's like to be confused.

We're not here to replace schools or colleges. We're here to fill the gaps they leave — with patience, clarity, and notes that actually help.

Because behind every rank, there's a student who just needed someone to explain it better.
